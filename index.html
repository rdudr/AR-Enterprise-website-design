Expert energy diagnostics

We measure first, so every recommendation is backed by data

Good savings start with accurate readings. Our team uses specialised instruments to see what is really happening inside your motors, pipes, panels and heat systems.

Thermal imagingSpots hot spots, insulation loss and loose electrical joints.

Vibration analysisFinds misalignment and bearing wear before breakdowns.

Acoustic analysisLocates compressed air and steam leaks by sound.

High-temperature flow measurementMeasures flue gas and hot fluid flows accurately.

Power quality assessmentChecks harmonics, power factor and voltage issues.

Thermal energy diagnosticsTraces where heat is lost across your process.
