Privacy & cookies

Last updated 27 October 2026

In short: this website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and shows no advertising. We only learn about you when you choose to contact us.

Our field apps, used by our own engineers on client sites, keep their data on the phone and on our own server only, also without cookies or tracking.

Who we are

AR Enterprise (AR Energy Services), Palanpur Canal Road, Surat 395009, Gujarat, India. For any question about your data, call +91 94262 59992 (Rahul Patel) or email energy_arenterprise@outlook.com.

Cookies

The website does not set any cookies, and it does not store anything in your browser. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to agree to.

The ARES Console, the internal workspace our staff sign in to, uses one strictly necessary cookie that keeps a signed-in staff member signed in. It cannot be read by scripts, is not used for tracking, and is removed when they sign out. It is never set for visitors of this website.

Content loaded from other services

To show the page, your browser fetches a few files from other services. Like any website request, this tells them your IP address and browser type. We do not send them anything else, and they set no cookies through our pages:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) for the typefaces.
  • Unsplash (images.unsplash.com) for some photographs.
  • Pixabay (cdn.pixabay.com) for the background video.

The map link and the WhatsApp button only open those services when you tap them.

When you contact us

The enquiry form does not send anything to us by itself. It opens WhatsApp with your message ready, and you decide whether to send it. Calls, emails and WhatsApp messages reach us through those services, under their own privacy terms.

We use what you send (your name, company, phone or email and message) only to reply to you and to prepare the work you asked about. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except where the work needs it (for example, an energy-audit certificate filed with an authority) or the law requires it.

Our field apps

Our engineers record plant readings in AR Enterprise Android apps. Those apps are for our team and clients' audits, not for the public. They:

  • keep the engineer's sign-in and the audit data in the phone's storage, so they work without internet;
  • send data only to our own server, api.arenergyservices.in, over an encrypted connection;
  • use no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

Client data from audits is used only for that client's audit and report. Our public pages never show client names; projects are described by industry type only.

How long we keep data

Enquiries are kept as long as we are in contact about the work, and then as long as our accounts and legal records require. Audit data is kept for the period agreed with the client and for any period the law requires for audit records.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it, by phone or email as above. We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
